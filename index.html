Register with NAV
nav.no · Done

Why it matters

NAV is Norway's labour and welfare agency. Registering unlocks job alerts, career guidance, and integration support. You're now in a recruiter-searchable database used by Norwegian employers.

Good to know

Registration takes ~10 minutes at nav.no. Requires BankID. Available in Norwegian and English.

🇳🇴 Norwegian work culture

NAV staff are trained to engage you as an equal. Direct questions and honest answers are expected — you don't need to be deferential or overly formal. Norwegians are comfortable with silence; don't fill pauses by over-explaining.

Registration · 02 of 03
Arbeidsplassen.no
National job board · Done

Why it matters

Arbeidsplassen.no is Norway's largest public job board, run by NAV. Most formal employers post vacancies here — it's the first place recruiters search for candidates.

Good to know

Set up job alerts for your STYRK field. A Norwegian-language profile gets significantly more recruiter views — use your My Match translations as a guide.

🇳🇴 Norwegian work culture

Norwegian job applications are concise. A two-page CV and a short, specific cover letter are the norm. Listing every responsibility reads as padding — Norwegian employers respond to outcomes and impact.

Registration · 03 of 03
HK-dir Godkjenning
Direktoratet for høyere utdanning

Why it matters

Norwegian employers may not recognise your degree without it. An HK-dir Godkjenning officially maps your qualifications to Norwegian standards — removing a key barrier for shortlisting. For IT roles (STYRK 2511/2512) it's strongly recommended.

Good to know

Apply early — approval takes 6–18 months. You can apply while job searching. Upload your confirmation here to get a verified badge on your profile immediately.

🇳🇴 Norwegian work culture

In Norway, a Godkjenning letter removes a specific uncertainty for employers — they don't always know how to evaluate a foreign degree without it. Bringing it up proactively in interviews signals self-awareness and preparation, which Norwegians respect.

Why it matters

B1–B2 Norwegian is the baseline for most client-facing IT roles in Norway. Even passive comprehension improves candidacy — colleagues switch to Norwegian in informal meetings.

🇳🇴 Norwegian work culture

Even basic Norwegian in professional settings signals respect and commitment. Switching to Norwegian in team meetings — even imperfectly — is noticed and appreciated. Norwegians won't correct you; they'll simply appreciate the effort.
